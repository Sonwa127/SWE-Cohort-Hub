GST 111 areas of concentration

Communication in English, 10 topics. These are 38 practice questions with answers worked out from the lecture slides, not an official answer key. Answers marked check are less certain, so confirm them with your lecturer or notes. Q9 and Q36 were missing from the slides.

1. Communication 10 questions
  1. We know the feelings and emotions of one another through ___.
    A partnership; B relationship; C communication; D education
    Answer: C. communication
  2. The two common factors in the definition of communication are ___ and ___.
    A sharing and exchanging; B feelings and thoughts; C speaking and listening; D writing and punctuation
    Answer: A. sharing and exchanging check
  3. The type of communication that involves gestures and body signs is ___.
    A verbal; B interpersonal; C non-verbal; D intrapersonal
    Answer: C. non-verbal
  4. ___ communication is the type that takes place within you.
    A personal; B intrapersonal; C interpersonal; D private
    Answer: B. intrapersonal
  5. Which type of communication pays attention to hierarchy and specific linguistic choices?
    A formal; B informal; C interpersonal; D verbal
    Answer: A. formal
  6. The medium through which a message is transferred is the ___.
    A channel; B vessel; C means; D feedback
    Answer: A. channel
  7. The last basic element of communication is the ___.
    A decoder; B receiver; C sender; D feedback
    Answer: D. feedback
  8. The first communication skill we acquire is ___.
    A reading; B writing; C listening; D speaking
    Answer: C. listening
  9. Communication skills are also known as ___ skills.
    A expressive; B receptive; C language; D productive
    Answer: C. language check
  10. Avoiding wordiness and observing brevity addresses which of the 7 Cs?
    A conciseness; B clarity; C completeness; D consideration
    Answer: A. conciseness
2. Writing 8 questions
  1. ___ is the expressive form of communication where the audience may be known or unknown.
    A speaking; B writing; C listening; D reading
    Answer: B. writing
  2. The two most important factors to consider before writing are ___ and ___.
    A medium and platform; B language and style; C audience and purpose; D time and space
    Answer: C. audience and purpose
  3. Which type of writing will you use to convince your best friend to enroll in MIVA Open University?
    A persuasive; B narrative; C descriptive; D expository
    Answer: A. persuasive
  4. Adamu included a list of references in the document he uploaded. What form of writing did he submit?
    A creative; B academic; C journalistic; D technical
    Answer: B. academic
  5. The three basic elements of writing skills are ___, ___ and ___.
    A Expressive, Presentation and Linguistics; B Linguistics, Composition and Presentation; C Presentation, Expressive and Composition; D Composition, Linguistics and Expressive
    Answer: B. Linguistics, Composition and Presentation
  6. The first stage of writing is known as ___.
    A drafting; B writing; C pre-writing; D post-writing
    Answer: C. pre-writing
  7. Peter submitted a term paper without citing the sources of his claims. He is guilty of ___.
    A regurgitating; B copying; C plagiarism; D piracy
    Answer: C. plagiarism
  8. Sit at the extreme end of the chair. The error in this sentence is known as ___.
    A redundancy; B ambiguity; C agreement; D reduplication
    Answer: A. redundancy check
3. Preposition 1 question
  1. The man ___ 'agbada' is my father.
    A on; B inside; C under; D in
    Answer: D. in
4. Sentences 5 questions
  1. Which of the following sentences suggests an invitation to Sussan?
    A Let's eat Sussan.; B Lets eat, Sussan.; C Lets eat Sussan.; D Let's eat, Sussan.
    Answer: D. Let's eat, Sussan.
  2. Which of the following is a compound sentence?
    A When Paul entered the class, the girl smiled.; B Paul, who was my friend, entered the class.; C Paul entered the class.; D Paul entered the class and the girl smiled
    Answer: D. Paul entered the class and the girl smiled
  3. Which of the following is not a coordinating conjunction?
    A or; B but; C because; D so
    Answer: C. because (it is subordinating)
  4. Such an amazing speech! This is an example of a/an ___ sentence.
    A declarative; B exclamatory; C interrogative; D imperative
    Answer: B. exclamatory
  5. The receiver of the action in a sentence is called the ___.
    A subject; B predicate; C complement; D object
    Answer: D. object
5. Phrase 1 question
  1. The house behind the church is ours. The underlined words (behind the church) are a/an ___ phrase.
    A noun; B prepositional; C adjectival; D verb
    Answer: B. prepositional check
6. Essay 2 questions
  1. Tope wrote about an incident that occurred in Wuse market last week. Her essay is a ___ type.
    A descriptive; B argumentative; C expository; D narrative
    Answer: D. narrative
  2. The thesis statement is found in the ___ part of an essay.
    A introduction; B body; C conclusion; D summary
    Answer: A. introduction
7. Paragraph 3 questions
  1. The logical connection of ideas in a piece of writing is known as ___.
    A coherence; B cohesion; C conciseness; D completion
    Answer: A. coherence check
  2. The unit of discourse in an essay is referred to as a ___.
    A sentence; B paragraph; C body; D transition
    Answer: B. paragraph
  3. The act of giving prominence to a single idea in a paragraph is referred to as ___.
    A completeness; B unity; C emphasis; D clarity
    Answer: C. emphasis
8. Transition 2 questions
  1. ___ phrases ensure a smooth flow and logical progression between paragraphs.
    A noun; B verb; C transitional; D prepositional
    Answer: C. transitional
  2. "Consequently, Jamiu passed in flying colours." The word 'consequently' in this expression is an example of ___.
    A adjectival; B nominal; C reflective; D transition
    Answer: D. transition
9. Verb 1 question
  1. The position of predicator in a sentence is occupied by a/an ___.
    A noun; B verb; C pronoun; D adverb
    Answer: B. verb
10. Comprehension 1 question
  1. The factors that affect comprehension are classified as ___ and ___.
    A linguistic and psychological; B linguistic and stylistic; C stylistic and psychological; D psychological and visualisation
    Answer: A. linguistic and psychological
Extra: adverbs, punctuation, abbreviations 4 questions
  1. I will pay you back ___.
    A installmentally; B installmentingly; C in installments; D installmentedly
    Answer: C. in installments
  2. ___ enough, I haven't been to the zoo before.
    A funny; B funningly; C funnily; D fun
    Answer: C. funnily
  3. The punctuation mark enclosed in the bracket ({ }) is known as ___.
    A ellipses; B braces; C caret; D semi-colon
    Answer: B. braces
  4. WAEC (West Africa Examination Council) is an example of a/an ___.
    A acronym; B abbreviation; C mnemonics; D meme
    Answer: B. abbreviation (it is read letter by letter, so not an acronym)

Choose or change your elective

Your exam is GST 125 or GST 127, whichever you registered for. Register through the Miva support portal.

  1. Open the Support Portal and sign in with Google using your student email. Set a login password if it asks.
  2. Go to My Zone, then Add a Ticket.
  3. Fill the form: Subject Course Registration. Classification Enrollments and Deferments. Case Add Course. Nature of Case Service Request.
  4. In the Description, write which elective you want: GST 125 Contemporary Health Issues, or GST 127 Environment and Sustainability. Submit and they process it.

Already registered and want to switch? Raise the same ticket and say you are changing from one elective to the other.
