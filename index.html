Choose or change your elective

Your exam is GST 125 or GST 127, whichever you registered for. Register through the Miva support portal.

  1. Open the Support Portal and sign in with Google using your student email. Set a login password if it asks.
  2. Go to My Zone, then Add a Ticket.
  3. Fill the form: Subject Course Registration. Classification Enrollments and Deferments. Case Add Course. Nature of Case Service Request.
  4. In the Description, write which elective you want: GST 125 Contemporary Health Issues, or GST 127 Environment and Sustainability. Submit and they process it.

Already registered and want to switch? Raise the same ticket and say you are changing from one elective to the other.
